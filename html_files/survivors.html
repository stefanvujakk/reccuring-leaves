Ray

v

Lore

v

A girl who had a normal childhood, besides her parents talking badly about her. She was very shy growing up and mostly kept to herself. At the age of 12 she got interested in: Experiments Basketball Boxing Poetry Geography & History At the age of 13 she started with small experiments... But at the age of 15 she made one decision that she would regret... Now she had made a little human-like creature. After a few months of keeping her, she got kind of attached, naming her XRay. But she got more and more aggressive every day, making Ray bring her to a forest and toss her into the water with tears in her eyes, knowing she would die... How wrong she was. A few years later, when Ray was 16/17, she met a few people separately and began forming a small group, making herself the leader. She grew especially attached to Timmy and Albi. A year later they planned a trip to the cabin where Ray’s grandfather used to spend most of his time, to stay there for a few nights. Ray thought XRay had died by now. She kept XRay a secret from her friends, knowing they would be freaked out. She only told Timmy and Albi, who kept quiet.
